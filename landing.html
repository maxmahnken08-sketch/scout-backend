Questions

Before you board.

The short version of everything people ask us.

Are the prices real?+

Yes. Scout searches live fares and rates — flights, hotels, trains, buses, cars and experiences — from real sources like Expedia, Google Flights, Kayak, Skyscanner and Booking.com. What you see is what you'd book.

Can I actually book inside the app?+

Hotels, yes — reserved without leaving Scout. For everything else, Scout opens a booking menu with the best sources for that exact leg — dates and fare already filled in, one tap from done.

What does the budget bar do?+

It re-plans the trip. Drag it down and Scout swaps in smarter flights, a leaner stay and cheaper eats; drag it up and the whole plan levels up — every piece re-chosen so the total fits your number.

What are the specialist agents?+

Scout splits the work across focused AI agents — one for flights, one for stays, one for food and experiences. Each digs deep on its own slice, and the results come back as one coherent plan.

Is Scout free?+

Scout is free to use. You only spend money on the trips you choose to book.

Which devices does it run on?+

Scout is built for iPhone, with a home-screen widget that counts down to your next trip.
